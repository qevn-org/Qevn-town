## 1. Architectural Overview & Design Intent

The QEVN Global Headquarters represents the premier architectural centerpiece of QEVN Town. Rather than a monolithic box or decorative primitive extrusion, the building is conceived as an interlocking composition of asymmetric contemporary volumes featuring:
- High-grade warm white architectural concrete frames with micro-beveled edges and panel reveals.
- Recessed double-glazed curtain walls with dark anodized aluminum perimeter framing and structural mullion grids.
- Warm horizontal teak/walnut architectural slat cladding providing organic warmth against the crisp concrete and tinted glass.
- A multi-tiered wrap-around concrete entrance podium with integrated granite steps and landscaped perimeter planter beds.
- A cantilevered executive boardroom volume projecting dramatically above the entrance portal.
- A fully activated rooftop sky terrace with teak decking, travertine outdoor dining table, modern armchairs, sun loungers, perimeter ficus trees, and mechanical penthouse (HVAC chillers, solar arrays, telecom mast).
- A visible interior reception lounge with minimalist desk, illuminated logo, floating cantilevered staircase, and warm pendant lighting.

## 3. Facade & Glazing Assembly

### 3.1 Primary Concrete Structural Framing
- **Material**: Ultra-high-performance architectural white concrete (`#F8F8F5`, Roughness: 0.78, Metalness: 0.05).
- **Bevel Profile**: 0.08m chamfer on all external edges to catch directional sunlight and ambient occlusion shadows.
- **Horizontal Reveal Joints**: 0.03m deep reveals every 3.2m representing floor slab casting intervals.

### 3.2 Glazing & Window Grid
- **Perimeter Frame**: Dark anodized architectural bronze/charcoal aluminum (`#1E2024`, Roughness: 0.32, Metalness: 0.85).
- **Mullion Grid**: Vertical mullions spaced at 1.8m intervals; horizontal transoms at 1.1m (chair rail height) and 2.6m (door head height).
- **Glazing Unit**: Double-pane low-E glass with subtle cyan-smoke tint (`#67E8F9`, Opacity: 0.42, Roughness: 0.08, Transmission: 0.88, IOR: 1.52).
- **Recess Depth**: Glazing is recessed 0.35m behind outer concrete framing, producing realistic shadow lines.

### 3.3 Accent Wood Cladding
- **Material**: Natural quarter-sawn warm teak (`#A05A2C`, Roughness: 0.52, Metalness: 0.04).
- **Configuration**: Horizontal slats (0.09m face with 0.02m shadow reveal) applied to:
  - Ground floor entrance flank wall.
  - Underside of cantilevered Level 3 boardroom soffit.
  - Sky penthouse vertical screening panels.

## 4. Ground Floor Hero Entrance & Podium

- **Wrap-Around Steps**: 3 continuous graduated tiers (Tread: 0.45m, Riser: 0.15m) running along front and side facades.
- **Entrance Canopy**: Cantilevered white concrete portal (Width: 6.8m, Projection: 2.8m, Slab Thickness: 0.4m) with 4 flush-mounted warm LED downlights (`#FEF08A`, 3000K).
- **Entry Doors**: Dual automated frameless glass sliding doors with vertical stainless steel pull handles (Height: 1.6m).
- **Security Bollards**: Four 316-grade brushed stainless steel cylindrical bollards (Height: 0.65m, Diameter: 0.12m) guarding pedestrian approach.
- **Landscaped Planters**: Heavy board-formed charcoal concrete planter troughs flanking entrance steps, containing rich mulch and sculptural boxwood shrubs.

---

## 5. Interior Depth & Visible Office Shell

Visible through the facade glazing to eliminate hollow appearances:
1. **Reception Counter**: Monolithic travertine slab with warm white oak waterfall edge and backlit warm LED reveal.
2. **Backdrop Feature Wall**: Vertical dark slat wall with illuminated "QEVN" brushed aluminum lettermark.
3. **Sculptural Staircase**: Floating cantilevered tread staircase rising from ground to second floor along East interior wall.
4. **Lighting**: Trio of brushed brass minimalist ring chandeliers suspended in the double-height atrium.
5. **Upper Floor Workspaces**: Silhouetted ergonomic desks, monitors with subtle interface glow, and acoustic partition fins.

## 6. Rooftop Sky Garden & Penthouse

- **Flooring**: Modular 0.6m × 0.6m weathered teak wood duckboard pavers with dark shadow joints.
- **Balustrade**: 1.1m high frameless tempered structural glass with slim satin stainless steel top cap.
- **Outdoor Dining Suite**:
  - Circular honed travertine dining table (Diameter: 1.8m).
  - 4 sculpted modern bucket armchairs in cream outdoor fabric.
  - 2 low-profile teak sun deck chaise loungers.
- **Vegetation**:
  - 2 specimen Japanese ficus trees in cylindrical white ceramic planter urns.
  - Linear perimeter planter box with ornamental fountain grasses.
- **Building Services Penthouse**:
  - Two dual-fan rotary HVAC chiller condensers with dark louvered air intake grilles.
  - Row of 8 monocrystalline photovoltaic solar panels tilted at 25 degrees.
  - Steel lattice communications mast with pulsing aviation obstruction beacon light.
